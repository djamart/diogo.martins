The rest of this site is about my work. This post isn't. If you're going to read what I write here, it seems fair that you know a bit about the person behind it.

## Taekwondo

I started taekwondo in 1999, and for a long time it was a big part of my life. I taught classes for ten years and also worked as a referee. That chapter is closed now, but after that many years it never quite leaves you.

## Guitar

I play guitar at what I'd call an intermediate level: good enough to enjoy it, not good enough for anyone to pay for it. Lately I've been learning jazz chords and bossa nova, which mostly means my fingers end up in shapes they didn't know they could make.

## Gaming

I used to play a lot of League of Legends and got to Platinum. These days I barely play. I didn't lose interest; the reason is further down.

## Running

I've run a few 10 km races. Then I took a long break, and now I'm trying to get back into a steady rhythm. It's humbling: the distance is the same as it always was, it just feels longer.

## Parenting, my main hobby

I'm the proud father of a little girl. I call parenting a hobby the way you'd call a full-time job with no days off a hobby. It's the one that swallowed all the others: fewer games, fewer runs, more bedtime negotiations. Best trade I've ever made.
